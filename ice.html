Apostle Spotlight

This project served an introduction to working with grids. The goal was just to make a grid that displayed all members of the general authorities. It wasn't too hard to do as just set a bunch of columns and adjust the size of individual items as needed

Favorite Devo

The goal here was learning how to work with tables. The idea was to just to create a table of devos, where they were, who was the speaker, the title and the date. It wasn't all that hard but formatting it to look pretty can take some practice

Prophet Cards

Here was making cards, mixing a series of items to make a nice card that people could look at for information. Sort of like what I have set up though less fancy.

Positioning Drills

This was an expansion to the apostle spotlight page. The goal here was to get a lot of experience with stacking items using grids. It was showing how an overlap can be set up and how we can structure the grids to achieve different things

Gallery

This was a far simpler goal. It was just getting used to compressing images for faster loading especially when using them as icons. It's not a challenge to really do and there isn't coding behind it really more just some commands/using some form of app
